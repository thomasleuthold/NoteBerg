Google Play release notes
=========================

Google Play allows max 500 characters per language, per release. Copy the
block for the release you are shipping into the Play Console (the <en-US>
tags are only needed when uploading notes as a file; when pasting into the
Console, paste the text between them).

Android-relevant items only. Windows and Nextcloud entries from
CHANGELOG.md are deliberately left out.

Only the current release is kept here; older release notes live in git history.


0.5.44 -- 498 characters
------------------------
<en-US>
- New: text boxes. Tap anywhere on the page to type there, drag to move, change the width. Works on PDF pages too.
- New: share a notebook with another Nextcloud user (preview, off by default in Settings).
- New: tasks get a name, status, priority and due date; filter and sort them in Markers.
- Edits made on two devices are now merged instead of one replacing the other.
- Exported PDFs keep your text's layout, are searchable, and show task checkboxes.
- New: Rate this app (Settings > About).
</en-US>
