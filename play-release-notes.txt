Google Play release notes
=========================

Google Play allows max 500 characters per language, per release. Copy the
block for the release you are shipping into the Play Console (the <en-US>
tags are only needed when uploading notes as a file; when pasting into the
Console, paste the text between them).

Android-relevant items only. Windows and Nextcloud entries from
CHANGELOG.md are deliberately left out.

Only the current release is kept here; older release notes live in git history.


0.5.43 -- 499 characters
------------------------
<en-US>
- New: backup and restore. Save all notebooks, notes, images, PDFs and recordings to one password-protected .zip file.
- New: camera Document mode outlines the page, straightens it and evens out lighting. Crop finds corners automatically.
- New: recording gain boost for quiet speech, and the same recording level on every device.
- Recordings are saved when you close the note or leave the app.
- Fixed spikes on fast strokes, transparent PNGs turning black, and two-finger pan turning into a zoom.
