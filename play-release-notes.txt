Google Play release notes
=========================

Google Play allows max 500 characters per language, per release. Copy the
block for the release you are shipping into the Play Console (the <en-US>
tags are only needed when uploading notes as a file; when pasting into the
Console, paste the text between them).

Android-relevant items only. Windows and Nextcloud entries from
CHANGELOG.txt are deliberately left out.


0.5.41 -- 466 characters
------------------------
<en-US>
- Lower drawing latency: ink follows the pen more closely.
- Faster scrolling and zooming in long notes and large PDFs.
- Wider pen pressure range, so light and firm strokes differ more clearly.
- Handwriting on imported PDFs is now exported as real PDF annotations
  instead of a flat image, so it stays sharp.
- Fixed the app closing by itself when syncing or zooming into large PDFs.
- Fixed zooming getting stuck.
- Fixed PDFs with JPEG 2000 images not opening.
</en-US>


0.5.39 -- 285 characters
------------------------
<en-US>
- Settings now open in a dialog instead of a full screen, and no longer
  overflow in portrait mode.
- Fixed the app being drawn underneath the system status bar.
- Fixed the whole app accidentally zooming while drawing.
- The build number is now shown next to the version in settings.
</en-US>


0.5.38 -- 249 characters
------------------------
<en-US>
- Handwriting and PDF pages now keep proper contrast in dark mode.
- Imported PDF pages can be inverted in dark mode so your annotations stay
  legible (Settings > Appearance).
- Fixed the note list becoming inconsistent when switching tabs quickly.
</en-US>


0.5.37 -- 202 characters
------------------------
<en-US>
- "Mark as Task" now works on every line, including the first line of a
  note and lines converted back from a list.
- Marking now applies only to the lines you selected.
- Clearer first-run help texts.
</en-US>


0.5.36 -- 303 characters
------------------------
<en-US>
- New: guided tours introduce the note editor on first use. You can replay
  them from Settings.
- Fixed a data-loss bug where "Mark as Task" on a selection spanning a
  list could blank the note.
- Fixed audio recording failing on a fresh install: the microphone
  permission is now requested properly.
</en-US>
